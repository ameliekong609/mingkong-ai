## Brand narrative

Ming combines more than ten years of experience in AI, data science and enterprise transformation with hands-on engineering of production GenAI and agentic AI systems.

Her differentiator is the ability to move between:

- executive-level business discovery
- enterprise AI architecture
- hands-on technical delivery

- organisational adoption and measurable business outcomes

She is particularly interested in AI systems that go beyond fixed LLM workflows, including persistent digital workers, multi-agent orchestration, tool use, reflective review, evidence grounding and controlled self-correction.
